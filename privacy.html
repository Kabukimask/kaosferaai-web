Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Who we are

Kaosfera ("we", "us") is a small studio that creates short educational videos about verified fun facts and publishes them to social media accounts. This policy explains what information the Kaosfera app collects when an account owner connects a TikTok or YouTube account, and how it is used. Contact: contacto@kaosferaai.com.

Information we collect

  • Account connection data. When you authorize Kaosfera through TikTok Login Kit or Google sign-in, we receive the data those services share with the permissions you grant: your account's basic profile (an account identifier, display name and avatar) and access tokens.
  • Posting settings. The caption, privacy level and interaction settings you choose for each video you decide to publish.
  • Video performance. Public statistics of the videos posted through Kaosfera (for example views and likes), used to improve future videos.

We do not collect your password, contacts, messages, location, payment details or any content from your account other than what is listed above.

How we use it

  • To upload and publish only the videos you preview and explicitly approve, to your own account.
  • To show you the options your account allows (privacy levels, comments, duets, stitches) before posting.
  • To measure how our own videos perform.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted, in private storage that only the Kaosfera system can read, and are used only for the purposes above. We keep them while your account remains connected.

Your choices and deletion

  • You can disconnect Kaosfera at any time from your TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions) or from your Google account (Security → Third-party access).
  • When you disconnect, or ask us by email, we delete your tokens and connection data within 30 days.

Children

Kaosfera is not intended for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and publish the new version on this page.

Resumen en español

Kaosfera solo recibe los datos básicos del perfil (identificador, nombre y foto) y los permisos que tú autorizas en TikTok o Google, para publicar en tu propia cuenta únicamente los vídeos que revisas y apruebas. No vendemos ni compartimos tus datos ni los usamos para publicidad. Los tokens se guardan cifrados. Puedes desconectar la app cuando quieras desde los ajustes de TikTok o de tu cuenta de Google, o escribirnos a contacto@kaosferaai.com, y borraremos tus datos en un máximo de 30 días.
